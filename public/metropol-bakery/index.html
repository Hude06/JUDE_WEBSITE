What we make

Breads & Rolls

Four simple ingredients — hard winter wheat flour, sea salt, water and sourdough starter developed with native yeasts.

Our Café

Yellow stucco walls, cozy banquette seating and pastry cases filled with beautiful, traditionally prepared cakes and pastries.

Special Occasion Cakes

Our fine pastry bakers combine their innate artistic talents with disciplined training to create cakes both beautiful and delicious.

Cakes, Pies & Desserts

Individually decorated cakes, unique in design — all our own fillings, frostings, purees and glazes, made in-house.

Viennoiserie, Pastries & Cookies

Croissants and classic French pastry made in the classic manner for over 40 years, laminated over 3 days and baked fresh every day.

Wheat-free Desserts

Rich cocoa soufflé cakes, mousse cups and more — available everyday, whole or by the piece.

A new chapter for a Eugene tradition

Founder Donna McGuinness ran Metropol for 48 years. In October 2025 she passed the ovens to longtime manager Maria Martin, who started here in 1998 and has done every job in the building. Same recipes, same standards, same bakery.
