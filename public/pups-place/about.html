ABOUT

A shop built around the dogs and cats who come in.

Pup's Place is locally owned and operated in downtown Springfield, Oregon. We're a grooming salon and a small retail pet store under one roof — and a lot of happy regulars in between.

A shop that grew up around its regulars.

Pup's Place started as a grooming salon on Main Street and slowly turned into something more. The retail side filled in around what customers kept asking us to carry — the right treats, the harness that actually fits, the calming chew that works on fireworks nights. Today we're still small on purpose. Grooming stays Fear Free. The shelves stay curated. The staff stays the same — which is why your pup greets Bianca like family and the regulars know which brand of treat just came in.
