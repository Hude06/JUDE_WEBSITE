HOW IT GOES

A visit to Pup's Place.

  1. 01

    Book or walk in

    Full grooms go through MoeGo — live availability, pick a time that works. Nail trims are walk-in any day we're open.

  2. 02

    Drop-off chat

    Two minutes at the counter. Tell us what you'd like done, how your pup is feeling, and any sore spots or sensitivities.

  3. 03

    Pick up pampered

    We text when your pup is ready. They leave with a fresh bandana, trimmed nails, and usually a new favorite treat from the shop.

Annie built Pup's Place around the pets who come in.

The grooming salon came first. The retail side grew up around what customers kept asking us to carry — the right treats, the harness that actually fits, the chew that works on fireworks nights. Today Annie and her crew run both sides of the shop out of the same little building on Main Street. We stay small on purpose. That's how Bianca can take the time a skittish cat needs. That's how every pup leaves with a bandana and a story.
