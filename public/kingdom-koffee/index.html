What sets Kingdom apart

Family owned, ground up

Founded by Jessica Kalashnikov and her family, sixteen years in Eugene before opening their first shop of their own.

Piroshki with a story

Sausage-and-mushroom and beef-and-cheddar, hand-folded and baked in-house — the thing everyone on College Hill asks for by name.

Journals, puzzles, and board games are always out. Order a piroshki and settle in — nobody's rushing you along.
