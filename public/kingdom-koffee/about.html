Our Story

About Kingdom Koffee

Kingdom Koffee opened on Eugene's College Hill after sixteen years of the Kalashnikov family calling this city home — it's the family's first shop of their own, started by local photographer Jessica Kalashnikov alongside her family.

The shop took over the former Off the Waffle space on South Willamette Street, in the same small plaza as Metropol Bakery, a short walk from Jade Dumpling & Noodle House and Wingstop.

What we do

Espresso, tea, and hand-folded piroshki — sausage-and-mushroom and beef-and-cheddar hand pies that nod to the family's Russian roots.

Why we do it

A space to love on our noble Eugene. Journals, puzzles, and games are always out for anyone who wants to stay a while.

Come sit with us on College Hill

Open Tuesday through Sunday — pull up a seat and stay as long as you like.
