## Sourcing notes

- Captions for posts 1–3 and 5–12 come from copy I read directly on
  judemakes.com (hero, work grid, How it works, About, Projects, Contact).
- Post 4 (Rosales Designs) is written from the work-grid thumbnail and its
  caption — the "16-year branding studio" line and the visible site copy
  ("Where Strategy, Creativity & Brand Identity Intersect", "Hi, I'm Patty
  Rosales", branding/logo work across food, lifestyle and consumer brands).
  I could not open rosalesdesigns.com from this environment, so it deliberately
  stays at that level of detail.
- There are **no posts for Bobby Salazar's or Gravel Truck Plans**. Their sites
  were unreachable here, so there are no screenshots to pair a caption with, and
  writing about a food gallery or a menu page I have not seen would be filler.
  Once the captures exist, the readable copy on their work-grid thumbnails is a
  starting point: Bobby Salazar's leads with "Salsa Made Simple" and a line
  about the business starting in 1990 with recipes going back generations,
  serving "at our Restaurants, in Retail and in Bulk"; Gravel Truck Plans leads
  with "For your family on their worst days."
